## Summary

Statistical-modelling and probability background should show that you can reason about uncertainty, sampling, estimation, assumptions, and decision risk. This is useful even when the final model is a machine-learning system.

## Example framing

A strong story might describe estimating conversion lift with confidence intervals, checking sample-ratio mismatch, segmenting effects, and deciding whether the effect was large enough to ship. That is better than only naming a statistical test.

## Experience map

Map each story to an uncertainty question: estimation, hypothesis testing, calibration, sampling bias, experimental design, or forecast intervals. Strong examples connect the statistical result to a decision, not just a formula.
