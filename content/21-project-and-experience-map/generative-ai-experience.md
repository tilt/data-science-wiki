## Summary

Generative-AI experience should demonstrate that you can design systems around model limitations, not only call an API. Good evidence covers retrieval, prompting, evaluation, safety, latency, cost, and human workflow integration.

## Example framing

A strong RAG project story explains how documents were chunked, indexed, retrieved, cited, evaluated, and updated. It should also explain what happened when the answer was not in the corpus and how hallucination or prompt-injection risks were tested.

## Experience map

Map each story to a system boundary: retrieval, context construction, structured output, tool use, evaluation, privacy, or cost. A credible generative-AI story explains what was deterministic outside the model and what was evaluated inside the model response.
