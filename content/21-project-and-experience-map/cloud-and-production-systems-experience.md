## Summary

Cloud and production-systems experience should demonstrate that you can run reliable data and AI workloads under real constraints: cost, permissions, latency, observability, deployment, and incident response.

## Example framing

A useful project story might say: a batch inference job moved from an ad hoc script to a scheduled container job with versioned inputs, retries, freshness checks, alerts, and rollback documentation. That shows production maturity more clearly than naming a cloud provider alone.

## Experience map

Map each story to a production concern: managed compute, storage, networking, observability, reliability, cost controls, incident response, or rollout strategy. The useful evidence is the tradeoff you made and how the system behaved when something failed.
