## Summary

Computer-vision experience should show how you handled visual data, annotation, model choice, evaluation, and deployment constraints. Good evidence includes representative failure cases, not only aggregate metrics.

## Example framing

A strong project story explains how segmentation labels were created, how ambiguous boundaries were handled, why a model architecture was chosen, and how performance changed under lighting or device differences.

## Experience map

Map each story to the visual task and the deployment risk: classification, detection, segmentation, OCR, domain shift, annotation quality, latency, or human review. The strongest examples name the visual failure modes you actually inspected.
