## Summary

Time-series forecasting experience should show how you turned historical observations into decisions under temporal uncertainty. Strong evidence includes baselines, validation design, forecast horizons, and how forecasts were used.

## Example framing

A useful story might explain how a seasonal baseline exposed that a complex model was not adding value, or how prediction intervals changed staffing or inventory decisions.

## Experience map

Map each story to the forecast horizon, validation protocol, baseline, uncertainty estimate, and downstream decision. Forecasting experience is strongest when it explains what changed because the forecast was trusted or rejected.
