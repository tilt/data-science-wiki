## Summary

Data-engineering experience should show that you can turn raw, changing data into trustworthy datasets, features, and analytical tables. The strongest stories explain contracts, quality checks, lineage, ownership, and recovery.

## Example framing

A strong story is not "built an ETL pipeline." It is: defined a data contract with upstream owners, transformed event data into dimensional tables, added freshness and volume checks, exposed lineage, and created a backfill process that avoided duplicate downstream records.

## Experience map

Map each story to a data contract, lineage path, quality check, warehouse model, orchestration decision, or backfill process. The important signal is not the tool name but how trust in the data was established and maintained.
