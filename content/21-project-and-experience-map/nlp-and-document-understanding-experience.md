## Summary

NLP and document-understanding experience should show how you convert messy language or documents into useful structure, decisions, or retrieval experiences. The hard parts are usually layout, ambiguity, entity resolution, evaluation, and workflow fit.

## Example framing

For an invoice extraction project, explain how pages were parsed, fields were extracted, vendor names were matched to records, low-confidence cases were reviewed, and extraction errors were measured by field importance.

## Experience map

Map each story to the language unit: token span, entity, document, table, page image, or workflow decision. The best examples explain how ambiguity was handled, not only which model was used.
