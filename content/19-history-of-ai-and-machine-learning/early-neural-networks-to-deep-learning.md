# Early Neural Networks to Deep Learning

## Summary

Deep learning emerged when neural-network ideas became trainable and useful at scale. The transition depended on data, compute, optimization, initialization, regularization, and software ecosystems, not only on adding more layers.

## Timeline

- Perceptrons and early neural networks established the idea of learned weighted units.
- Backpropagation made multi-layer networks trainable in principle.
- Practical progress was limited by compute, data, activation choices, initialization, and optimization difficulty.
- GPUs, large datasets, better nonlinearities, normalization, and regularization made deep models effective across vision, speech, language, and recommendation.

## Historical mechanism

The transition to deep learning depended on more than deeper networks. Better optimization, GPUs, larger datasets, initialization, normalization, and regularization made multi-layer models trainable and useful at scale.
