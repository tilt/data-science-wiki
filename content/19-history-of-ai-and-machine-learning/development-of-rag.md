# Development of RAG

## Summary

RAG developed from combining information retrieval with neural generation so models could answer using external, updateable evidence rather than only parametric memory.

## Timeline

- Classical search systems retrieved documents but did not synthesize fluent answers.
- Neural retrievers and dense embeddings improved semantic matching beyond exact lexical overlap.
- Large language models made fluent synthesis practical, but their parametric memory was stale and difficult to audit.
- RAG connected retrieval, context construction, and generation so answer quality could depend on a governed corpus.

## Historical mechanism

RAG joined two older threads: information retrieval systems that find evidence and language models that generate fluent text. The historical lesson is that generation quality depends on retrieval quality, corpus governance, and answer verification, not only on model scale.
