# Statistical Pattern Recognition to Modern Machine Learning

## Summary

Modern machine learning inherits much of its language from statistical pattern recognition: features, classifiers, decision boundaries, estimation, generalization, and evaluation on held-out data.

## Timeline

- Statistical pattern recognition framed prediction as assigning observations to classes under uncertainty.
- Classical methods such as nearest neighbors, linear discriminants, decision trees, kernel methods, and graphical models emphasized features and decision rules.
- The machine-learning era broadened the toolkit with scalable optimization, ensembles, representation learning, and large empirical benchmarks.
- Deep learning shifted more feature construction into the model, but still relies on the same concerns: sampling, generalization, error analysis, and deployment shift.

## Historical lesson

The field did not replace statistics with engineering. It layered optimization, computation, data scale, and representation learning on top of statistical questions about uncertainty, evidence, and decision quality.
