## Summary

Answer patterns help turn technical knowledge into concise interview responses. They provide structure for definitions, comparisons, system design, debugging, and tradeoff questions.

## Core patterns

For concept questions, define the term, give intuition, state assumptions, and name failure modes. For comparison questions, compare objective, data, strengths, weaknesses, and use cases. For system design, clarify requirements, propose architecture, evaluate, and discuss operations.

## Example

For "What is calibration?" a strong answer defines predicted probabilities versus observed frequencies, gives an example, explains why it matters for thresholds, and mentions reliability diagrams or calibration error.

## Practice rule

Use patterns to organize thinking, not to sound scripted. The answer should still adapt to the question and point to concrete examples from projects when possible.
