## Summary

Knowledge-gap tracking identifies concepts that need review before an interview or project discussion. It turns vague anxiety into a concrete study backlog linked to canonical wiki pages.

## How to use it

Record the question, the missing concept, the canonical page to review, a short explanation in your own words, and one example. Revisit the gap until you can answer without reading the page.

## Example

If you cannot explain why random train-test splits leak in forecasting, link the gap to rolling-origin validation, write a two-sentence explanation, and practice a demand-forecasting example.

## Prioritization

Prioritize gaps that appear across many interview themes: evaluation, leakage, uncertainty, system design, deployment, and failure modes. Low-frequency trivia should come later.
